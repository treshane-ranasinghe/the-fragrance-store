    The Art of Scent

    Every fragrance tells a story in three acts

    I

    Top Notes

    The first 15 minutes

    The opening overture — bright, fleeting and vivid. Citrus, bergamot, pink pepper and aromatic herbs that draw you in.

    II

    Heart Notes

    Up to 4 hours

    The soul of the perfume. Rose, jasmine, iris and spice unfold as the top notes fade, revealing its true character.

    III

    Base Notes

    Lingers all day

    The lasting memory. Oud, amber, vanilla, musk and precious woods that stay on skin long after you've left the room.
